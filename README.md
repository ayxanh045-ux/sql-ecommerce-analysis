# SQL E-commerce Analysis (Olist Dataset)

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes a real-world e-commerce dataset (~100,000 orders) using SQL to understand business performance, customer behavior, and product trends.

The goal is to extract actionable insights that can help improve revenue, customer retention, and product strategy.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used

* SQL (MySQL)
* Dataset: Olist Brazilian E-commerce Dataset

---

## 📌 Key Analysis Performed

### 🔹 Revenue Analysis

* Calculated total revenue
* Analyzed monthly revenue trends
* Identified peak sales periods

### 🔹 Customer Analysis

* Identified top customers by total spending
* Analyzed repeat customers and retention rate (~3–5%)
* Evaluated customer contribution to revenue

### 🔹 Product Analysis

* Found top-selling products
* Identified highest revenue-generating products
* Analyzed product category performance

---

## 📈 Key Insights

* Revenue shows growth over time with peak periods in later months
* A small number of customers generate a large portion of total revenue
* Repeat customer rate is low, indicating poor customer retention
* Certain product categories dominate overall sales

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* Invest more in top-performing product categories
* Promote high-revenue products through targeted marketing
* Improve customer retention through loyalty programs and incentives
* Provide special offers to high-value customers to increase engagement
